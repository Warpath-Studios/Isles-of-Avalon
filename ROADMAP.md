# Isles of Avalon — Public Roadmap

This roadmap gives players a high-level view of what we're working on. It is organized by gameplay area, not by internal development tasks, and it does **not** include release dates or timelines unless we've explicitly announced them elsewhere.

Status values used below:

- **Planned** — designed or scoped, not yet in active development
- **In Development** — actively being built
- **Testing** — being verified internally or with test players
- **Released** — live in the current build
